# Sample photos — sources and licences

Every photo in this folder is free to use in this app and its
screenshots. None of them depicts a real person who has not consented:
the people are invented, and no photo carries a brand logo or artwork
from any game.

- `boulevard.jpg`, `pier.jpg` — made with an image model for make.fun
  and shipped in the gta6-cover-art-01 kit's starter, whose rules say
  the two sample photos "were made for make.fun with an image model
  and are free to use".
- `convertible.jpg` — generated with an image model for this entry
  (Muse media pipeline, 2026-11-19): a woman with sunglasses leaning
  on a vintage red convertible on a palm boulevard at golden hour.
- `rooftop.jpg` — generated with an image model for this entry
  (Muse media pipeline, 2026-11-19): a man in a floral shirt on a
  hotel rooftop at dusk, skyline and palms behind him.
- `neon-street.jpg` — generated with an image model for this entry
  (Muse media pipeline, 2026-11-19): two friends laughing on a
  neon-lit night street, wet pavement reflecting pink and teal neon.
- `marina.jpg` — generated with an image model for this entry
  (Muse media pipeline, 2026-11-19): a lifeguard tower and marina
  masts at sunset.

Generated images were saved as JPEG and lightly compressed (long side
capped at 1600 px) so the app stays small; no other edits were made.
