# Sample photo sources

All six sample photos are license-free assets bundled so the studio opens
with a finished cover. None carries a licence fee, attribution duty or
usage restriction for this entry.

- `boulevard.jpg`, `pier.jpg` — supplied with the make.fun
  gta6-cover-art-01 starter kit as free sample assets; copied in from the
  kit's `app/samples/` directory.
- `convertible.jpg` — AI-generated for this entry: a woman with big
  sunglasses leaning on a vintage red convertible on a palm boulevard at
  golden hour. No real person is depicted.
- `rooftop.jpg` — AI-generated for this entry: a bearded man in a floral
  shirt on a hotel rooftop at dusk. No real person is depicted.
- `neon-street.jpg` — AI-generated for this entry: two friends laughing on
  a neon-lit night street. No real people are depicted.
- `marina.jpg` — AI-generated for this entry: a lifeguard tower and marina
  masts at sunset.

Fonts are separate: Anton and Pacifico are used under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1; their licence texts sit beside the font files in `app/fonts/`.
