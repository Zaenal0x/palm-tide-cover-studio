# Sample photo sources

All six photos bundled in this folder are license-free assets that ship with
the app so a fresh cover works offline, with no network requests.

- `boulevard.jpg` — provided by the gta6-cover-art-01 starter kit as a free
  sample asset (palm boulevard scene).
- `pier.jpg` — provided by the gta6-cover-art-01 starter kit as a free sample
  asset (pier at dusk scene).
- `convertible.jpg` — AI-generated for this entry in the build session
  (woman with sunglasses leaning on a vintage red convertible, palm
  boulevard, golden hour). No real person, no logos, no text.
- `rooftop.jpg` — AI-generated for this entry in the build session (man in a
  floral shirt on a hotel rooftop at dusk, skyline and palms). No real
  person, no logos, no text.
- `neon-street.jpg` — AI-generated for this entry in the build session (two
  friends laughing on a neon-lit street at night, wet pavement). No real
  people, no logos, no text.
- `marina.jpg` — AI-generated for this entry in the build session (lifeguard
  tower and marina masts at sunset). No logos, no text.

The generated images were produced with the build environment's image
generation tooling and are used here as original, license-free artwork.
Any photo a user uploads replaces these samples panel by panel.
